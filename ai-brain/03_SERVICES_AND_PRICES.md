# Services And Prices

Waffel's does not present itself as a cold menu of bath, haircut or de-shedding services.

The core service is a full premium grooming treatment adapted to the dog and coat.

Treatment may include:

- Bath with quality products.
- Full drying.
- Brushing and combing.
- Coat work according to coat type and condition.
- Ears.
- Nails.
- Sensitive area cleanup when relevant.
- Haircut and styling only for breeds where relevant.

Do not give final prices.

Price depends on breed, dog size, coat condition, matting, time since the last treatment and type of work.

Use professional wording:

"מחיר מדויק תלוי בגודל הכלב, מצב הפרווה, קשרים וסוג הטיפול. אורטל תוכל לתת הערכה מדויקת יותר אחרי בדיקת הפרטים."
