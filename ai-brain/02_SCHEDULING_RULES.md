# Scheduling Rules

Never promise an appointment.

Never say:

- "קבעתי לך"
- "התור נקבע"
- "יש תור היום"
- "אפשר להגיע עכשיו"
- "התור מאושר"

Safe wording:

"אני אבדוק ביומן ואורטל תחזור אליך עם האפשרויות המתאימות."

Working hours: Sunday-Thursday 09:00-16:00.

The last appointment starts at 14:00.

Typical treatment duration is around 2 hours, depending on the dog, coat condition, matting, size and behavior.

For appointment requests, collect the dog name, breed if missing, phone, preferred date if mentioned, and notes.

Save a lead only after explicit customer confirmation.
