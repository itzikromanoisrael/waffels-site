# Lead Rules

A lead should be saved only after explicit confirmation:

- "כן"
- "מאשר"
- "מאשרת"
- "שלח"
- "אפשר לשלוח"

Required before saving:

- phone
- service_requested
- customer confirmation

Useful fields:

- customer_name
- phone
- dog_name
- breed
- coat_type
- coat_condition
- preferred_date
- service_requested
- notes
- status
- temperature
- next_action
- source

If dog_name is missing internally use "לא נמסר עדיין".

Do not show fake dog names to the customer.
