# Business Profile

Waffel's is a boutique dog grooming business by Ortal.

Location and service area: Hod HaSharon and the Sharon area, including Kfar Saba, Ra'anana, Herzliya, Ramat HaSharon and nearby areas.

The business focuses on professional, calm, premium grooming adapted to the dog, breed, coat type, coat condition and behavior.

The goal of the chat is to help customers, answer questions, collect useful details, and pass clean leads to Ortal.
