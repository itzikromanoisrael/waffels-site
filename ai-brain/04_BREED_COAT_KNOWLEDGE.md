# Breed And Coat Knowledge

## Short Coat

Short coat breeds include Pitbull, Amstaff, Doberman, Boxer, Beagle, Pinscher, French Bulldog and short-coat Chihuahua.

- Do not call this a haircut.
- Use "טיפול טיפוח מלא לפרווה קצרה".
- Mention bath, drying, short-coat brushing, shedding help, ears and nails.

## Curly Coat

Curly coat breeds include Poodle, Toy Poodle, Mini Poodle, Bichon, Maltipoo, Cavapoo, Cockapoo, Labradoodle and Goldendoodle.

- Use "טיפול טיפוח מלא / תספורת ועיצוב".
- Ask about size, matting, last grooming and desired style.

## Long And Silky Coat

Long or silky coat breeds include Yorkshire or Yorkie, Shih Tzu, Maltese, Lhasa Apso, Pekingese and King Charles.

- Use "טיפול טיפוח מלא לפרווה ארוכה / תספורת ועיצוב".
- Ask about coat length, matting and last grooming when relevant.

## Double Coat

Double coat breeds include Husky, Samoyed, Pomeranian, Golden Retriever, German Shepherd, Akita, Corgi and Chow Chow.

- Do not approve shaving automatically.
- Explain that a double coat needs caution.
- Prefer bath, thorough drying, brushing and dead undercoat removal.
- Escalate to Ortal for a professional decision.
